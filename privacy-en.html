MedCoach Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

MedCoach ("App", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have.

1. Who We Are

MedCoach is developed by Vasyl Zadvornyy, individual entrepreneur, Ukraine.

Contact: vasyl.zadvornyy+medcoach@gmail.com

2. What Data We Collect

Account data:

  • Email — for authentication
  • Name and profile photo — if you sign in with Google

App data:

  • Your created reminders — titles, descriptions, dosages, schedules
  • Reminder completion events — when you tap "Done", "Skipped", or snooze
  • Device push token — for delivering notifications
  • Activated habit packs

Temporary AI processing data:

  • Prescription photos — processed by Google Gemini and immediately discarded
  • PDF documents — same
  • Voice recordings — same
  • AI parsing text input — same

What we DO NOT collect:

  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Access to other apps
  • Biometric data
  • Apple Health / Google Fit data

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Providing the service — creating and delivering reminders
  • Account authentication and security
  • AI parsing of your photos/PDFs/text/voice into structured reminders
  • Computing personal statistics for your use
  • User support via email

We do NOT use your data for:

  • Advertising
  • Selling to third parties
  • Marketing profiling
  • Government surveillance (without lawful basis)

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following services to run MedCoach:

  • Supabase Inc. (US/EU) — database and authentication. Privacy Policy
  • Google LLC (Gemini AI) — processing your photos/PDFs/text/voice. Data is processed and immediately discarded, not used for model training (per Gemini API terms). Privacy Policy
  • Google LLC (Sign In) — Google account authentication. Privacy Policy
  • Apple Inc. — iOS push delivery via APNs. Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase (FCM) — Android push delivery. Privacy Policy
  • Expo Inc. — push routing. Privacy Policy

5. Data Storage

Your data is stored in Supabase (hosted in EU and US). We retain your data while you actively use the App.

If you delete your account, all your data is permanently erased within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

As a MedCoach user, you have the right to:

  • Access: view all your data in the App
  • Correction: edit or delete any data you created
  • Account deletion: complete data removal via Settings → Delete Account, or by emailing vasyl.zadvornyy+medcoach@gmail.com
  • Data export: request export of all your data via email
  • Withdraw consent: stop using the App
  • File a complaint: to the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights if you believe your rights have been violated

7. Security

We implement technical measures to protect your data: TLS encryption in transit, controlled database access, authentication via secure OAuth protocols.

No service is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but do everything technically feasible.

8. Children

MedCoach is not intended for users under 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you discover that a child has created an account, please contact us and we will remove the data.

9. Medical Disclaimer

MedCoach is an informational tool for personal health management. The App:

  • Is NOT a medical service
  • Does NOT replace consultation with a healthcare provider
  • Does NOT provide diagnoses
  • Does NOT recommend dosages

Always follow your doctor's recommendations and consult with healthcare professionals about your health.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy. For significant changes, we will notify you via email or in-app notification. The last updated date is at the top of this document.
