MedCoach Data Deletion

Developer: Vasyl Zadvornyy (individual entrepreneur)

MedCoach gives you full control over your data. This page explains how to delete your account entirely or specific data categories.

Ways to delete data

📱 In the app (recommended):
  • Individual reminders: Swipe reminder left in list → "Delete"
  • Pause reminders (recoverable): Open reminder → "Pause"
  • Delete entire account: Menu "More" → "Delete account" → double confirmation

Request via email

If you prefer to delete data via email — write to:

medbotsupport@gmail.com

Include in your email:

  • Subject: "MedCoach Data Deletion Request"
  • Account email to be deleted
  • What to delete: entire account, or specific categories (reminders, AI processing history, statistics)

We will process your request within 30 days and send confirmation to your email.

What data will be deleted

When deleting entire account, we remove:

  • User profile (email, name from Google Sign In)
  • All reminders and their history
  • Reminder completion records (for statistics)
  • Activated habit packs
  • App settings
  • Device push token
  • AI parsing credits
  • AI request metadata (without document contents — those are never stored)

Data that may remain temporarily

  • Database backups are retained for up to 30 days for disaster recovery, then automatically deleted
  • Server logs are kept for up to 14 days for diagnostics (contain only IP and timestamp, no request content)

After 30 days from deletion, your data is fully destroyed from all systems.

Data that is never stored

Some data is processed instantly and never stored on our servers:

  • Prescription photos you send for AI parsing
  • PDF documents for AI processing
  • Voice recording transcripts

These files are sent to Google Gemini, processed there, return a result — and are immediately discarded from our system. Per Gemini API terms, Google also does not use them for model training.
